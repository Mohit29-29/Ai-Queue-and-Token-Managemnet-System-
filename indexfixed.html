About Smart Queue System

Revolutionizing Public Office Queue Management

The DRCC Smart Queue Portal is an AI and IoT-integrated public queue management system designed to eliminate long waiting lines at District Registration and Counseling Centers (DRCC) and Bank branches.

Integrated with real-time Firebase Cloud Databases and ESP32-CAM hardware counters, the portal dynamically calculates service times based on specific work requests—ensuring smooth queue flow, digital security verification, and zero crowding.
